How this works

  1. These are your keypads, drawn the way they'll look on the wall in your project's finish (marked ★).
  2. Tap any keypad to change what the buttons say, or give that one keypad a different finish. A name turns red if it's too long to engrave; shorten it or add a space so it fits on two lines.
  3. Your changes save on this device as you go, so you can come back to this link later.
  4. Check off each keypad with the ✓ in its corner once it's right. That approves it and locks it so it can't change by accident; tap the ✓ again to unlock it. You can also approve a whole floor, or every keypad at once.
  5. When you're done, tap “Send changes” to email them to us.

You can tap other finishes at the top to compare. To pick a different one, tap it, then “Use … for my project”.
